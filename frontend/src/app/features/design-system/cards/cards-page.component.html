Book card

The new app-book-card in its states. Covers render as placeholder jackets — hue and title-only vs title+author are seeded from the book, so nothing is bundled and nothing reshuffles.

The card emits intents; consumers wire them to the reader, menus, and selection. Open a card's context menu from its kebab or by right-clicking the cover. Last output fired: {{ lastEvent() }}

Rest, selected, selection mode

At rest: cover, series badge, one progress bar, title + author. Hover a card to reveal the action pill, the kebab, and the checkbox live. The middle card toggles its selection ring; the right-hand card is in selection mode, so every checkbox stays visible and a plain click toggles.

Context menu · plain user

The kebab and right-click open the same shared context menu. Gated verbs are absent, not disabled: this card is wired for a user with no permissions, so it shows only Add to shelf and Mark as. The cards above carry the full menu (Send, Download, Metadata, Delete).

Placeholder jackets

A seeded gradient with the title set in a system serif — title+author and title-only compositions, different hash hues. Hover a card: the text block lifts clear of the overlay verbs.

Dashboard variants

The same anatomy with a few extras: an always-visible action, an eyebrow line, a recency line, or everything switched off for the book-page strip. Audiobook heroes carry a headphones glyph top-right.

Series cards

The artwork is the deck: the first cover carries the card, the next two step up and behind it, each darkened. The pile silhouette is the whole "this is a series" signal — no badge. Progress on the front cover is the read fraction; a single-book series drops the back layers and reads like a book. Hover for Continue (Start when nothing's read); click opens the series page. Series have no menu of their own yet, so the kebab stays hidden.

Author cards

A circle forgives the wildly inconsistent provider photos; when a photo is absent or fails to load the card falls back to a quiet two-letter monogram. Authors have no menu of their own yet, so the kebab stays hidden. No match badge at rest — matching is the facet's job. The checkbox and selection ring work exactly like the book card.

Skeletons

The zero-data instance: a cover block plus two meta lines at the real card line heights, with a slow low-contrast shimmer (static under reduced motion). The ~180ms delayed reveal — mount the skeleton only if data hasn't committed yet, and flush it the moment it does — belongs to the consumer, not the card.
